    cell-area integration. `--data-psf`/`--cell-psf gaussian` (sigma user-set, not nside-derived).

    `weight` column is **not consumed downstream**.

  - `geospatial.py` — `export_healpix_to_geotiff` writes a **regular GeoTIFF** (GTiff/deflate,
    nearest-neighbor equirectangular resample), not a COG. `healpix_to_geodataframe`/
    `save_healpix_to_geoparquet` + boundary caching present.
  - DuckDB used **internally** (aggregate loading, sidecar stats) but no showcase example.
  - No Zarr/GeoZarr, UXarray, xdggs, mocpy, COG, DuckDB showcase, or gallery datasets.
